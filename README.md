# Cold Start

Cold Start is a 3D first- and third-person technology company simulator. Wake up in an abandoned IT facility, restore its power, claim it as your HQ, and build a company from nothing.

## Game Concept

You discover an abandoned IT facility filled with server rooms, offices, networking equipment, power systems, cooling infrastructure, storage areas, and forgotten technology. The facility is not your inherited company or headquarters—you discover it abandoned and decide to turn it into the foundation of a completely new company.

The game combines physical exploration with deep company simulation. You can walk through the facility in first person for close interaction or switch to third person for exploration and seeing your character in the world.

### Core Loop

1. Explore the abandoned facility.
2. Restore essential power and infrastructure.
3. Inspect, repair, clean, and renovate the building.
4. Establish the first operational areas of your company.
5. Purchase equipment and physically place it in the facility.
6. Set up offices, laboratories, server rooms, and employee spaces.
7. Hire employees and organize departments.
8. Develop technology, products, and services.
9. Manage finances, infrastructure, maintenance, and expansion.
10. Grow from one abandoned building into a large technology company.

## Planned Systems

- First-person and third-person character gameplay
- Physical interaction with the environment
- Explorable 3D facility
- Power generation and distribution
- UPS and backup systems
- Cooling and environmental management
- Servers and data-center infrastructure
- Networking equipment
- Offices and laboratories
- Purchasing and equipment delivery
- Construction, renovation, and expansion
- Employees and departments
- Research and development
- Products and services
- Company finances and operating costs
- Facility maintenance and failures
- Progression from a small startup to a major technology company

## Design Philosophy

The world should feel physical. Important actions should happen in the environment rather than being reduced to management menus. Equipment occupies real space, rooms have practical purposes, infrastructure has dependencies, and the player's headquarters visibly changes as the company grows.

The abandoned facility is the starting point, not the entire game. As the company develops, the player can expand the original headquarters and eventually operate additional offices, laboratories, warehouses, and data centers.

## Technology

- Engine: Unity 6 (6000.0.62f1)
- Genre: 3D simulation / management / exploration

- Target platform: PC

## Development Status

Early concept and prototype stage. The initial goal is a small, playable 3D environment with character movement, camera switching, physical interaction, and the first pieces of the abandoned facility.

More complex company and infrastructure systems will be introduced incrementally.
